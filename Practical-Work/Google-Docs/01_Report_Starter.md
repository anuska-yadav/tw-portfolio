## Introduction

## Uses of Technology in Education
Digital learning platforms provide access to course materials and assignments. Video meetings and collaborative documents support communication. Simulations and interactive resources can help learners explore concepts.

## Benefits
Technology can make learning resources easier to access, support collaboration, and allow students to review material at their own pace. Digital documents also make editing and feedback more convenient.

## Challenges
Students may face distractions, unequal access to devices or internet connectivity, and privacy concerns. Technology is most useful when it supports clear learning goals and responsible teaching practices.
